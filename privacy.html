Privacy policy

Effective 7 October 2026

This policy explains what the FitFocus Android app ("FitFocus", "we") collects, why, where it is kept and how you can delete it. We collect only what the app needs to work. We do not sell your information, show ads, or use advertising or analytics trackers.

Information stored with your account

When you create an account, these are stored in our cloud database so you can use them on any phone you sign in to:

  • Sign-in details: your email address and, if you use email sign-up, your password. Passwords are stored only as a secure hash by our authentication provider; we cannot read them.
  • Google sign-in: if you choose "Continue with Google", Google shares your name, email address and Google account ID with FitFocus. We never see your Google password.
  • Profile: the name you choose and your training preferences (goal, experience level, session length and weekly target).
  • Profile photo: only if you add one. It is resized on your phone and stored privately; only your account can access it.
  • Workout history: the guided workout plans you complete, with date, time, active time and your optional effort rating.
  • Exercise safety notice: the date you accepted it, so it is not shown again after you reinstall.

Information that stays on your phone

  • Camera: the form coach analyses camera images on your phone. Images are never uploaded or sent to us or anyone else.
  • Session videos and reports: a video of your camera session with tracking lines, and its analysis, is saved on your phone only and deleted automatically after 24 hours. You can delete it sooner.
  • Practice totals and cached data: camera practice totals, and copies of your profile and workouts kept so the app works offline, stay on the phone.
  • Android backup is turned off for FitFocus, so this data is not copied to cloud backups.

Permissions

FitFocus asks for the camera only when you start the form coach, and uses the internet to sign in and sync your account. You can refuse camera access and still use the rest of the app.

How we use your information

  • To sign you in and keep your account secure.
  • To recommend workout plans that match your preferences and recent activity, and to show your progress.
  • To send emails you request, such as account confirmation and password reset.

Workout recommendations are made by simple rules in the app, not by sharing your data with any AI service.

Who we share it with

We do not sell or rent your information. It is processed only by the services that run the app:

  • Supabase: hosts our authentication, database and private photo storage (servers in the Asia-Pacific, Mumbai region).
  • Google: only if you choose Google sign-in, to confirm your Google account.

We may disclose information if the law requires it.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted HTTPS connections. Database rules ensure each account can read and change only its own records and photo.

How long we keep it

Account information is kept until you delete your account. Session videos and reports on your phone are deleted after 24 hours.

Deleting your account

In the app, open You and tap Delete account. After you confirm, your account, profile, profile photo and workout history are permanently deleted from our servers, and FitFocus data for that account is removed from the phone. If you cannot use the app, email us and we will delete it for you.

Your choices

You can view and edit your name and preferences in the app, remove your profile photo at any time, and delete your account. To ask about the information we hold about you, contact us.

Children

FitFocus is not intended for children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Health information

FitFocus gives general fitness guidance only. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose or treat any condition.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the app.
