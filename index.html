Build strength. Find your focus.

FitFocus is an Android fitness app with workouts shaped around your goal, experience and available time, and a camera form coach that runs entirely on your phone.

What the app does

  • Recommends workout plans and explains why each one was picked.
  • Guides you through each exercise with illustrated movement guides.
  • Counts reps and gives form cues for four exercises using your phone's camera. Camera images are processed on the phone and are never uploaded.
  • Shows your weekly progress and recent sessions.
